Bottle Design

SQUEEZIT’s bottle design is inspired by the natural shape of a fruit, featuring a rounded, squeezable form that fits comfortably in the hand. The design emphasizes playfulness and functionality, with a wide opening for easy filling and cleaning. The bottle is made from squeezable plastic, allowing users to control the flow of juice with a simple squeeze. The surface is printed directly with product information and branding using washable ink, eliminating the need for additional labels and enhancing recyclability. This thoughtful design approach not only creates an engaging user experience but also supports sustainability by reducing material waste.
