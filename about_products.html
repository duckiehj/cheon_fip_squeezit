Product Overview

SQUEEZIT is a 100% real fruit squeezed juice made to deliver bold, tropical flavour in every sip. Crafted from vibrant fruits and designed for everyday refreshment, it brings a clean, fruit-forward taste with a bright finish.

Boosted with added Vitamin C, SQUEEZIT supports daily energy while keeping the experience light and enjoyable. Designed to be held and pressed, it turns a simple drink into a refreshing, active moment.

Bottle Design

The SQUEEZIT bottle is soft, flexible, and made to be squeezed—turning every sip into a playful, hands-on experience. Its ergonomic shape allows juice to flow smoothly with a simple press, making it easy and interactive to enjoy anywhere. Designed with minimal materials and direct washable printing, the bottle reduces excess packaging while maintaining a clean, functional form.
